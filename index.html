01 / Introduction

About me

My academic interests lie at the intersection of finance, economics, and higher education. I hold a bachelor's degree in finance from Auburn University and an MBA from Belmont University. My long-term goal is to contribute to university research and teaching.

I am especially interested in the financial decisions that affect universities and the students they serve—from tuition and financial aid to public appropriations and institutional budgeting.

Questions worth asking

My interests center on how universities manage finite resources while expanding opportunity and sustaining institutional quality.
